How to read this. Figures are returns over variable costs per acre, before land, machinery ownership and labor. Farm-only and dual figures use one formula: crop yield × price, minus input costs. Under panels, yield is scaled by the share of sunlight the crop receives, and input costs shrink by a chosen share of the lost yield. This is a planning estimate, not a lease offer or financial advice.
